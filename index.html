TrackIt · Todo & Remember

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

TrackIt: Todo & Remember is an offline productivity app. It does not require an account and does not send app data to a TrackIt server. We do not operate a backend for the app.

Information on your device

Tasks, descriptions, completion status, daily productivity scores, profile name, theme, and images you select are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Past task images and tasks are removed when you delete them or when their day is finalized. Finalized daily percentages are kept in the app's local database. The app does not collect analytics or advertising identifiers.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, TrackIt schedules notifications on your device. Android may request notification and exact-alarm access so reminders can appear at the selected time. The app does not send reminder content to a server.

Email and sharing

The feedback and progress email actions open the email app you choose. TrackIt passes the message and recipient to that app only after you tap the email action. The email app and its provider then handle the message under their own privacy terms. TrackIt does not send email in the background.

Photos

If you choose a profile or task photo, it is copied into TrackIt's private app storage and stays on your device. You can remove it by replacing or deleting the associated photo or item.

Data removal

You can delete tasks and photos from within the app. Removing TrackIt from your device deletes its private app data. Export or back up any information you want to keep before uninstalling; daily score history is not transferred to another device.

Children's privacy

TrackIt does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. No account or online service is provided.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated with future app releases. For privacy questions, contact the developer through the email address shown on the TrackIt Google Play listing.
